The Time Traveller was explaining something difficult to his guests, and the fire was kind enough to make everyone patient. Space, he said, has three dimensions that we move through as we please. Time is a fourth, and we drift along it at a steady pace whether we like it or not.

"But why should we not travel in it the way we travel in the others?" he asked.

The Medical Man smiled. The Psychologist frowned. The Very Young Man, who had been waiting for an excuse to be astonished, leaned forward.

To prove his point, the host brought out a small model: a glittering frame of nickel and ivory, with a saddle and a pair of levers. He pressed one lever, and the little machine shivered, grew faint, and was gone.

Nobody spoke for a while. The Editor was the first to suggest a trick with mirrors, and the Time Traveller laughed and promised a better demonstration the following week.

***

When the guests returned the next Thursday, the host was not there. Dinner was laid, the fire was lit, and the empty chair at the head of the table seemed to be listening.
