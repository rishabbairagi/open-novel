Ilsa Varn kept two ledgers. The first belonged to the Calloway Sky Company and recorded cargo, fuel, and passengers. The second belonged to Ilsa and recorded everything the company preferred not to know: the places where the aether thinned, where compasses sulked, and where the wind seemed to have opinions.

On the morning the *Marigold* left Port Stray, the second ledger was heavier than the first.

"You're humming," said Odo, the engineer, from somewhere inside the boiler housing.

"I'm calculating."

"You hum when you calculate. It sounds like a kettle losing an argument."

She ignored him and opened the chart table. Under the usual maps lay a sheet she did not remember filing: thin paper, brown ink, a coastline she had never flown. Along its margin ran a dozen blue lines, curved like the veins of a leaf.

***

They were aether currents, and every one of them was uncharted.
